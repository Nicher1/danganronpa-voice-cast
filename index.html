CHAPTER PREDICTIONS

Victim and Blackened voting

Every actor now receives two predictions per chapter. They can move them freely before the Trial; entering the Trial permanently locks that chapter's choices.

RESULTS & MEMORIAL

Persistent results without double scoring

Pre-Trial deaths can establish the Victim, the host can mark the Blackened from Memorial, and prediction markers stay visible after a character dies. Result snapshots prevent duplicate points.

STATISTICS

Actor records and known population

The new Statistics tab tracks correct Victim guesses, correct Blackened guesses, and historical character deaths separately. Its host-controlled chart shows Alive at the bottom, Dead above it, and Unknown on top.

TRIAL & VOICE SAFETY

Chapter-aware sample sources

The chapter selector tracks the chapter currently being played. Outside a Trial, living characters use the previous chapter's best samples. During a Trial, they use only spoiler-safe pre-trial samples from the current chapter.

SYNC & ACCESS

Shared, role-protected data

Predictions, results, graph values, and statistics persist through Supabase. Players can edit only their own current pre-Trial predictions; spectators remain read-only; graph and result controls remain host-only.
